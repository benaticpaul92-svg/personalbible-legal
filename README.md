# Privacy Policy - Personal Bible

**Effective April 15, 2026**

## Summary

Personal Bible is built around your name and family. We collect what we need to personalize scripture for you - and nothing else. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with anyone except the named processors below.

## What we collect

- Your first name (required) - used to insert into verses.
- Optional: family member names (spouse, children, parents) - used in family-promise verses.
- Optional: email address and phone number - only if you provide them in your profile.
- Subscription status - managed by Apple via StoreKit; we receive only your entitlement state, never your payment details.
- Anonymous usage analytics (see "Analytics" below).
- Crash reports (see "Crash reporting" below).

## Where it's stored

- All profile data is stored locally on your device by default.
- If you sign in to enable cloud backup, your profile and saved verses are also stored on Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider. Data resides in AWS region ap-southeast-1 (Singapore), chosen for global latency balance and compliance with international data-protection standards. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). You can sign out and delete this data at any time.
- We do not transmit your name, family names, contact details, or saved verses to any other third party.

## Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand which features are used so we can improve the app. Events captured include: which screens you open, how often you start a prayer session, and whether you save a verse. We do NOT capture your name, family names, contact details, or the text of any verses.

Firebase Analytics may collect a device identifier, country, language, and OS version. You can disable analytics entirely from your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

## Crash reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to receive automatic reports when the app crashes. Crash reports include the exception type, the stack trace, and basic device info (model, OS version, free memory). We strip personally identifying information before sending.

No personal data, no verse text, and no profile content is included in crash reports.

## Notifications

With your permission, we schedule local notifications on your device to remind you of your daily verse and prayer time. These are scheduled by iOS itself - they do not pass through any of our servers. You can disable notifications at any time from your device's Settings > Notifications > Personal Bible.

## Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see your payment information. We receive only your entitlement state (whether you have an active subscription) so we can unlock Pro features.

All subscription management - cancellation, refund requests, family sharing - is done in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device.

## Your rights

Under GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), and equivalent international privacy frameworks, you have the following rights:

- **Access:** your profile is stored on your device and is visible in Profile > My Name and Profile > My Family. To request a copy of any cloud-stored data, contact martin@jebnengineering.com.
- **Deletion:** you can clear your local profile by reinstalling the app. If you have signed in for cloud backup, you can delete your cloud data by signing out and selecting "Delete my account data". To request expedited deletion, contact martin@jebnengineering.com - we respond within 30 days as required by law.
- **Portability:** contact martin@jebnengineering.com and we will provide a JSON export of all your data within 30 days.
- **Withdraw consent:** you can disable analytics, notifications, and cloud sync at any time from the Profile screen.

## Children

Personal Bible is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK, per GDPR). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact martin@jebnengineering.com and we will delete it within 7 business days.

## International users

Personal Bible is available globally. Wherever you use the app, we apply the same baseline privacy standards described in this policy. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, and other jurisdictions with specific privacy laws have additional rights as described in "Your rights" above. By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your data to AWS region ap-southeast-1 (Singapore) for cloud backup, where applicable safeguards are in place.

## Changes to this policy

When we materially change this policy, we update the in-app version number and surface a banner asking you to review the changes. The current policy version is 1.

## Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or to exercise your rights under GDPR, CCPA, or equivalent frameworks, contact martin@jebnengineering.com. We respond within 7 business days.
